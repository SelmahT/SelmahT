### 👩🏾‍💻 About me

Hey! I'm Selmah, a data science student in Nairobi. I like projects where the data is messy, the problem is real, and the result is something a person can open and use. Most of what's here started as a question I couldn't stop thinking about.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** [MamaCare Kenya](https://github.com/SelmahT/maternal-health-risk-kenya), my final year project on maternal health risk
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** deep learning, and better ways to explain model predictions
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Bayesian modelling, Power BI and DAX, fine-tuning translation models, Streamlit apps
- 📐 **Strong suit:** statistics and mathematics, from calculus and Bayesian inference to risk modelling in R
- 🪵 **Fun fact:** I play woodball, a sport most people have never heard of
- 🗣️ **Languages:** English, Kiswahili, Spanish, and a growing interest in Dholuo, Ekegusii and Somali through my NLP work

### 📐 Statistics & mathematics

The maths is what I enjoy most, and it shows up in how I build things:

| Area | Where I've used it |
|---|---|
| **Bayesian inference** | Hierarchical (partial-pooling) models of hospital charges, compared with a flat baseline using WAIC; Bayesian credible intervals in MamaCare Kenya |
| **Risk modelling** | A risk modelling project in R from my Mathematical Finance course; 3-class maternal health risk classification |
| **Statistical learning** | Classification with XGBoost and Scikit-learn, class balancing with SMOTE, and model evaluation |
| **Model explainability** | SHAP values to explain individual predictions |
| **Calculus & algebra** | Peer tutor in Calculus & College Algebra |
| **Association & pattern mining** | Apriori on transaction data (support, confidence, rule interpretation) |

### 🔄 How I work

1. **Understand the question** before touching the data
2. **Clean and document** every transformation, so it can be repeated
3. **Model with the right tool**, and compare against a simple baseline
4. **Explain the result**, not just report a score
5. **Ship something usable**, like a dashboard or an app
